Privacy Policy Delete account

Delete your Kashio account

Delete your account instantly in the app, or request deletion by email. You do not need to install, reinstall or sign in to the app to submit an email request.

Delete in the app

While signed in, tap your avatar and choose Delete account, or open Settings → Privacy and account → Delete account. Read the confirmation and tap Delete permanently.

The account is deleted on the server straight away and every session ends: that phone is signed out at once, and other phones are signed out the next time they contact the server. If the app says it couldn't confirm the deletion, it may already have completed. Try again; if the app then signs you out, the account was deleted. You can also use the email route below.

Or send a deletion request by email

Open deletion-request email

This opens an email draft; you must send it. Clicking the link does not submit a request or delete anything.

If no email app opens, use any email service to write to noreply.kashio@gmail.com with the subject Kashio account deletion request. Say that you want your Kashio account and associated data deleted.

Send from the email address you used to sign in to Kashio when possible. If you no longer have access to it, contact the same address for an alternative ownership check.

Ownership verification

We verify that a request comes from the account owner before removing data. Support will explain any verification needed. Never send your password, sign-in code, API key, bank account number, raw SMS or transaction export. Reinstalling the app is not required to request verification.

What account deletion removes

Deletion removes your account identity and associated email, optional name, username, phone number, sign-in identities, pending sign-in codes and every session on every device. It is not a temporary freeze or just signing out. Your username becomes available to others, and your account will no longer authorize parser/model downloads.

Ask about any other data you previously sent to support. A later sign-in may create a new account; it does not restore the deleted profile.

Data that may need separate retention

If some records must be retained for a legitimate security, fraud-prevention or legal reason, we will explain the data, reason and applicable retention period or criteria in our response. Backups and external processors may have separate expiry cycles; we do not promise instant erasure of every copy.

See the retention section of the Privacy Policy. Deletion is not fulfilled merely by revoking access while retaining an otherwise unnecessary account profile.

Financial records on your phone

Your transaction database stays on your phone and is not backed up by your account, so deleting the account, in the app or by email, does not erase it. Neither can erase data on an offline phone or copies you exported. Tell support if you also need guidance on removing local records.

To remove app-local data yourself, uninstall Kashio or open Android Settings → Apps → Kashio → Storage and clear storage. This is irreversible and can erase your only transaction history, settings and saved keys; create a backup first only if you want to keep a copy. This step is not required to submit an account-deletion request and does not itself delete your server account.

Independently saved backups, reports, emails and copies held by recipients are not automatically deleted. Removing an AI API key or signing out is also not account deletion.

What happens next

In-app deletion is immediate. For email requests, support will verify ownership, explain the expected handling time and any necessary retention, and confirm the outcome; this website itself does not delete anything. If you have not received a reply, follow up at noreply.kashio@gmail.com.
